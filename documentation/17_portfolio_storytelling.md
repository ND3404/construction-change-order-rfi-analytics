# Portfolio Storytelling Guide

## One-sentence description

A construction workflow analytics case study that identifies RFI and change-order bottlenecks, commercial exposure, and project-level management priorities.

## Interview narrative

1. I began with a construction business problem rather than a tool.
2. I designed a relational synthetic dataset because public data did not contain the required linked workflows.
3. I intentionally included raw-data quality issues and documented every cleaning decision.
4. I built SQL views and analytical tables for RFI, change, workflow, and project risk.
5. I tested relationships and exploratory models while separating association from causation.
6. I translated results into executive, operational, and portfolio products.
7. The project demonstrates how construction project-management experience and analytics skills work together.

## Credibility controls

- All data is synthetic.
- Results are not an industry benchmark.
- The risk score is transparent.
- The model is not approved for production.
- Management recommendations will be created in the Act phase.
