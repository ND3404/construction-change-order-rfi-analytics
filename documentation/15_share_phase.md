## Principal messages

- RFI and change-order performance should be reviewed as connected workflows.
- Slow owner-decision profiles perform worse across both workflows.
- High digital coordination is associated with stronger RFI response performance.
- Coordination conflicts create the largest RFI cost impact.
- Commercial exposure is concentrated in owner-directed, design error/omission, and unforeseen-condition changes.
- Revision loops and old pending items are practical management intervention points.
- Submission-only RFI attributes do not provide a reliable production prediction of later change linkage.

## Communication controls

- Retain the synthetic-data disclosure.
- Do not claim actual client results.
- Do not present correlations as causation.
- Do not describe exploratory models as production AI.
- Display both volume and duration when interpreting bottlenecks.
- Reconcile all published KPI values to the validated SQLite database.
