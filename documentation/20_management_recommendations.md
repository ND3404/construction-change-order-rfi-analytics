# Management Recommendations

## 1. Launch a Red-project recovery program

Review all 29 Red projects, beginning with the top 10. The project manager should validate each indicator, assign corrective actions, and report weekly until the score falls below 6.

## 2. Treat High/Critical overdue RFIs as decision exceptions

Every High or Critical RFI overdue more than seven days should have an accountable decision owner and a documented due date. Field-impact RFIs require immediate sequence-protection actions.

## 3. Establish a weekly change aging board

The board should review:

- all changes older than 35 days;
- high-value pending changes;
- exposure above 1.5% of project budget;
- incomplete commercial packages;
- and decisions requiring executive escalation.

## 4. Enforce forecast-incorporation discipline

Approved changes should be incorporated into forecasts within five business days. Reconcile the approval register, cost forecast, contingency use, and schedule forecast weekly.

## 5. Introduce quality gates

### RFI quality gate

Require:

- project location;
- drawing or specification references;
- one clear question;
- impact statement;
- required decision date;
- and proposed response path.

### Change-package quality gate

Require:

- scope narrative;
- cost breakdown;
- schedule analysis;
- supporting RFIs or directives;
- authorization basis;
- and requested decision.

## 6. Improve decision cadence

Create recurring owner-decision sessions with complete pre-read packages. Slow owner-decision projects showed weaker performance across both RFI and change workflows in the synthetic portfolio.

## 7. Target coordination conflicts

Coordination Conflict was the largest RFI cost-impact category. Use monthly Pareto reviews, discipline ownership, and model-based coordination to prevent repeated issues.

## 8. Pilot digital coordination

Select Low digital-coordination projects for a controlled pilot. Measure response time, on-time rate, revision loops, and change linkage before expanding the approach.

## 9. Automate only governed exceptions

Automate transparent alerts for workflow health, overdue RFIs, old pending changes, exposure, and forecast gaps. Do not automate decisions or use the exploratory model in production.

## 10. Measure realized benefits

Report actual performance at 30, 60, and 90 days. Benefits must be supported by observed metrics and should not be assumed from recommendations alone.
