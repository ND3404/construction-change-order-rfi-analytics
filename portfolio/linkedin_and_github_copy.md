## LinkedIn launch post

I have completed my second end-to-end construction analytics portfolio project:

**Construction Change Order and RFI Analytics — Root-Cause, Cycle-Time, and Impact Analysis for Construction Decision Workflows**

The project analyzes 3,318 RFIs, 1,119 change orders, workflow-event histories, and explicit RFI-to-change relationships across 90 synthetic construction projects.

Key results include:

- Average RFI response: 12.21 days
- RFI on-time rate: 45.7%
- Approved change value: $204.57 million
- Pending change exposure: $88.10 million
- Average change approval cycle: 28.91 days
- Project workflow health: 29 Red, 48 Yellow, and 13 Green

The strongest tested portfolio relationship was between project-average RFI response time and project-average change approval cycle, with Pearson r = 0.817. This is an association, not proof of causation.

The completed project follows the Ask–Prepare–Process–Analyze–Share–Act lifecycle and includes terminology aligned with CRISP-DM, DMAIC, Microsoft’s data-science lifecycle, and PMI project-governance process groups.

Tools and methods:

Excel | SQL | SQLite | Python | Statistical Analysis | Power BI | Tableau | Construction Project Controls | Workflow Analytics

All data is synthetic and does not represent actual clients or confidential construction records.
